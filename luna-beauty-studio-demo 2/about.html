Our Story

A two-chair studio that grew slowly, on purpose — and never traded the calm for the crowd.

Est. 2019 · Quezon City

It started with one honest rule

In 2019, Luna opened with two chairs, one lamp, and a rule that everything else would follow from: never rush a client. We booked fewer slots than we could have sold, spent longer on each set than was strictly profitable, and explained every product we used before we used it.

Word travelled the way it does in a neighbourhood — slowly, then all at once. By 2021 we'd added lash and brow services. By 2023, a dedicated skin room. Today the studio runs seven signature services across four disciplines, with a small team that still recognises regular clients by name.

What hasn't changed: the hygiene standard, the transparent pricing, and the breathing room in every appointment.

What We Stand For

Studio values

🤍

Comfort first

Soft lighting, warm towels, no loud music. You should be able to relax or even doze off during your appointment.

🔬

Hygiene without compromise

Instrument sterilisation, single-use files and buffers, fresh linens per client. No exceptions, no shortcuts on busy days.

🌿

Gentle formulations

Low-odour gels, cruelty-free lashes, and skin-conscious products chosen for sensitive clients as much as for results.

💬

Honest advice

If a service isn't right for your nails, lashes, or skin, we'll say so — and suggest what will work instead.

📖

Educated clients

We explain aftercare properly, every time: how to keep a gel set, how to sleep on fresh lashes, what to avoid after a brow tint.

⏳

Time, respected

Your slot is yours. We don't double-book and we don't stack appointments back-to-back just to fill the calendar.

Our Philosophy

Service is a craft, not a transaction

A manicure is thirty minutes of someone's attention. A lash set is ninety. We think the correct response to that gift of time is care — which is why we ask about your day, remember your preferences, and never let the studio get so busy that the work gets sloppy.

Every service at Luna begins with a short consultation and ends with an aftercare brief. The middle — the actual work — is where the standards live.
